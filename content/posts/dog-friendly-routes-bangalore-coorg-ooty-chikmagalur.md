The drive is half the adventure. Here is how to plan Bangalore's three favourite hill trips with your dog in the car.

## Bangalore to Coorg

- **Distance:** about 250 to 270 km
- **Time:** 5 to 6 hours
- **Route:** Mysuru Road to Mysuru, then on through Hunsur or Kushalnagar to Madikeri

**Stops:** the stretch to Mysuru has plenty of places to pull over. Plan a longer break around Mysuru, before the final leg.

**Watch out for:** busy weekend traffic leaving Bangalore. Leave early in the morning when it is cooler and roads are clearer.

## Bangalore to Ooty

- **Distance:** about 270 to 300 km
- **Time:** 6 to 8 hours
- **Route:** via Mysuru and Gundlupet, through Bandipur and Mudumalai

**Watch out for:**

- **Forest roads:** Bandipur and Mudumalai are tiger reserves. Night traffic is restricted, and stopping or getting out inside the forest is not allowed. Give your dog a good break before you enter the forest stretch.
- **Hairpin bends:** the climb into Ooty has many sharp bends. Dogs prone to car sickness struggle here. Feed lightly before the climb and drive slowly.
- **Cold:** Ooty can be cold at night. Short-haired and senior dogs may need a jacket and extra bedding.

## Bangalore to Chikmagalur

- **Distance:** about 240 to 250 km
- **Time:** about 5 hours
- **Route:** via Hassan on the Mangaluru highway, then up to Chikmagalur

**Stops:** good highway stretches with places to pull over, and a sensible break at Hassan before the hills.

**Watch out for:** the last climb has winding roads. Stop before it for water and a short walk.

## Rules for every route

1. **Stop every two to three hours.** Water, a short leashed walk, a toilet break.
2. **Never leave your dog in a parked car,** even with windows open.
3. **Keep your dog leashed at highway stops.** Traffic is fast and stray dogs are common.
4. **Carry water from home.** Sudden changes can upset some dogs' stomachs.
5. **Check for ticks** at the end of each day.
