Bangalore's three favourite hill getaways — **Coorg, Ooty and Chikmagalur** — are all within a day's drive. With a dog in the back seat, the journey needs a little more planning than usual: where to stop, how to handle the ghat roads, and which stretches have rules you can't break.

Here's how to plan each route, with a checklist that works for all three.

## At a glance

| Route | Distance | Drive time | Difficulty with a dog | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bangalore → Coorg | 250–270 km | 5–6 hours | Easy to moderate | Weekend traffic out of Bangalore; monsoon leeches |
| Bangalore → Chikmagalur | 240–250 km | ~5 hours | Easy to moderate | Winding final climb; motion sickness |
| Bangalore → Ooty | 270–300 km | 6–8 hours | Moderate to hard | Forest closures; 30+ hairpin bends; cold nights |

## 🌳 Bangalore to Coorg

- **Distance:** about 250 to 270 km
- **Time:** 5 to 6 hours
- **Route:** Bangalore–Mysuru Expressway to Mysuru, then Hunsur and Periyapatna to Kushalnagar and Madikeri
- 🗺️ [Open this route in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Bengaluru&destination=Madikeri%2C+Karnataka&waypoints=Mysuru%7CKushalnagar&travelmode=driving)

### Where to stop

- **Before the expressway:** get your dog walked and toileted before you leave. The expressway is fast, with limited places to pull over.
- **[Around Mysuru](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Mysuru):** the natural mid-point for a longer break, water and a proper stretch.
- **[Kushalnagar](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kushalnagar):** a last break before the climb into Coorg's coffee country.

### Plan around

- **Leave early.** Weekend traffic leaving Bangalore builds fast after 7 am, and mornings are cooler for your dog.
- **Monsoon (June to September):** Coorg is beautiful but very wet. Leeches are common on estate trails, and thick-furred dogs stay damp for days. Pack extra towels and check paws and between toes after every walk.
- **Unfenced plantations.** Coffee estates go on for acres. Keep your dog on a leash — a dog chasing a squirrel can be lost in minutes.

## 🌲 Bangalore to Ooty

- **Distance:** about 270 to 300 km
- **Time:** 6 to 8 hours
- **Route:** Bangalore–Mysuru Expressway to Mysuru, then Nanjangud and Gundlupet, through the Bandipur and Mudumalai tiger reserves, then up the ghat to Ooty
- 🗺️ [Open this route in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Bengaluru&destination=Ooty%2C+Tamil+Nadu&waypoints=Mysuru%7CGundlupet%7CGudalur&travelmode=driving)

### The forest stretch: rules you can't break

The road from Gundlupet to the Nilgiris runs through **Bandipur (Karnataka) and Mudumalai (Tamil Nadu) tiger reserves**.

- **Night closure:** traffic through the reserves is closed at night, typically **9 pm to 6 am**. Time your trip to cross in daylight.
- **No stopping:** you can't stop, get out or feed animals inside the reserves. Give your dog a long break at [Gundlupet](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Gundlupet) before entering the forest, so nobody needs a toilet stop for the next hour.
- **Windows up near wildlife.** An excited bark at an elephant is not a good idea for anyone.

### The climb into the Nilgiris

- **Hairpin bends:** the ghat roads into Ooty have dozens of sharp bends. The Kalhatty ghat via Masinagudi is shorter but very steep, with 30-plus hairpins, and has had restrictions on vehicles in the past; the Gudalur route is longer but gentler. Check current road status before you leave.
- **Car sickness:** this is where dogs prone to nausea struggle. Feed lightly well before the climb, crack a window, drive the bends slowly and stop at a safe spot if your dog starts drooling or yawning a lot.
- **Nilgiris e-pass:** tourist vehicles entering the Nilgiris have needed an e-pass since 2024. Check whether it applies to your dates and apply online before you go (search "TN e-pass Nilgiris" for the official portal).

### In Ooty

Ooty gets cold, especially at night and in winter. Short-haired, small and senior dogs may need a jacket and extra bedding.

## ☕ Bangalore to Chikmagalur

- **Distance:** about 240 to 250 km
- **Time:** about 5 hours
- **Route:** NH75 (Mangaluru highway) via Nelamangala, Kunigal and Hassan, then up to Chikmagalur
- 🗺️ [Open this route in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Bengaluru&destination=Chikkamagaluru%2C+Karnataka&waypoints=Kunigal%7CHassan&travelmode=driving)

### Where to stop

- **[Kunigal area](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kunigal):** a sensible first break about 1.5 hours in.
- **[Hassan](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hassan%2C+Karnataka):** the main stop before the hills — water, a walk and a toilet break. If you have time, [Belur](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chennakeshava+Temple+Belur) is a short detour.

### Plan around

- **The last climb** into Chikmagalur has winding roads. Stop before it, and take it slowly.
- **Cooler weather, fewer crowds:** Chikmagalur is often calmer than Coorg, which suits older or anxious dogs.

## 🐾 Rules for every route

1. **Stop every two to three hours.** Water, a short leashed walk, a toilet break.
2. **Never leave your dog in a parked car** — not even for five minutes with the windows open. Cars heat up dangerously fast, even on cloudy days.
3. **Leash at every highway stop.** Traffic is fast and community dogs are common at dhabas and toll plazas.
4. **Secure your dog in the car** with a harness and seatbelt clip, a crate, or a back-seat barrier. Never let them ride with their head out of the window.
5. **Carry water from home.** A sudden change of water can upset some dogs' stomachs.
6. **Check for ticks every evening,** especially after walks in grass, estates or forest edges.
7. **Save the number of a vet** near your destination before you set off. In Bangalore, keep our [vet hospitals by area guide](/vet-hospitals-bangalore-by-area/) handy.

## Signs your dog needs a break now

Excessive drooling, repeated yawning or lip-licking, whining, pacing, panting that won't settle, or vomiting. Pull over safely, let your dog out on a leash, offer a little water and give them ten minutes. If the signs return, shorten the day's drive. Heavy panting with pale gums or collapse is an emergency — cool your dog down and find the nearest vet.
