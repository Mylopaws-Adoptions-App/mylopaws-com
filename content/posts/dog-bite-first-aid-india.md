Dog bites are a daily reality in India. The National Centre for Disease Control recorded **over 37 lakh dog bite cases in 2024**, and Karnataka alone reported **2.86 lakh dog bites and 26 suspected rabies deaths** between January and August 2025. India accounts for an estimated 36% of the world's rabies deaths.

The good news: **rabies is almost 100% preventable** with the right first aid and prompt vaccination. This guide covers what to do if **you** are bitten, and what to do if **your dog** is bitten by another dog.

> **Bitten right now?** Wash the wound with soap under running water for 15 minutes, apply an antiseptic, and go to a doctor or government hospital today. Don't wait to see if the dog seems healthy.

## Part 1: If a person is bitten by a dog

### Step 1: Wash the wound for up to 15 minutes

This is the single most important thing you can do — it physically removes the virus and bacteria.

- Wash under **running water with soap** (any household soap or detergent) for **up to 15 minutes**.
- Wash gently into puncture wounds.
- Do this even for small scratches that break the skin.
- **Arrived late?** Wash anyway. India's guidelines say wound cleaning is still needed even if you reach a doctor late.

### Step 2: Apply an antiseptic

After washing, apply an antiseptic such as **povidone-iodine** solution or ointment, or an alcohol-based antiseptic.

### Step 3: Don't cover tightly, don't use home remedies

- Leave the wound **uncovered** or use a loose, clean dressing.
- **Never apply** chilli, turmeric, lime, oil, coffee powder, herbs or any other home remedy. Doctors stress that no traditional remedy prevents or treats rabies — they can worsen infection and delay real treatment.
- Doctors usually **avoid stitching** bite wounds immediately, because closing the wound traps infection.

### Step 4: See a doctor the same day

Go to a doctor, hospital or government health centre **the same day** — and start vaccination within 24 hours. Tell them:

- When and where the bite happened, and where on your body
- Whether the dog was a pet or a community dog, and if it's vaccinated
- Whether you've had rabies vaccines before (carry your record)

**Bites on the face, head, neck or hands are the most urgent.** The virus can reach the brain faster from these areas.

## Which antibiotic or antiseptic cream to use on a dog bite?

People often search for the "best antibiotic cream for a dog bite". The honest answer:

- **First aid:** an antiseptic like **povidone-iodine** after thorough washing.
- **Antibiotics should be decided by a doctor.** Dog bites carry many bacteria, and deep punctures often need **oral antibiotics** — usually for 3 to 7 days — not just a cream.
- **A cream does not protect against rabies.** Only vaccination and, where needed, rabies immunoglobulin do.

Your doctor will also check whether you need a **tetanus** booster.

## Rabies vaccine after a dog bite: what to expect

### How doctors grade the exposure

Indian guidelines classify exposures into three categories, based on WHO guidance:

| Category | Type of contact | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **I** | Touching or feeding an animal, licks on unbroken skin | Wash the area. No vaccine needed |
| **II** | Nibbling of bare skin, minor scratches or abrasions without bleeding | Wound care + full course of anti-rabies vaccine |
| **III** | Bites or scratches that break the skin, licks on broken skin, saliva on eyes, mouth or nose | Wound care + full vaccine course + **rabies immunoglobulin (RIG)** |

### The vaccine schedules used in India

| Schedule | How it's given | Days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Intramuscular (Essen)** | One dose into the upper arm muscle | **0, 3, 7, 14 and 28** |
| **Intradermal (updated Thai Red Cross)** | Small doses into the skin at two sites | **0, 3, 7 and 28** |

Day 0 is the day of your first dose. **Complete every dose.** Stopping halfway leaves you unprotected.

### Rabies immunoglobulin (RIG)

For Category III bites, doctors also give **rabies immunoglobulin** — ready-made antibodies that protect you while the vaccine takes effect. As much as possible is injected **into and around the wound**. It's given once, at the start of treatment.

### If you've been vaccinated before

If you've had a full rabies vaccine course before (as treatment after a bite, or as pre-exposure vaccination), you'll usually need only **two booster doses, on days 0 and 3**, and **no immunoglobulin**. If you completed a documented full course very recently, your doctor may advise wound care alone. **Carry your vaccination record** — doctors need proof.

### Free treatment in Karnataka

Karnataka provides **anti-rabies vaccine and rabies immunoglobulin free of cost** at government health facilities — primary health centres, community health centres, taluk hospitals and district hospitals. Under the state's 2026 rabies action plan, private hospitals must also give immediate care without asking for advance payment. Rabies has been a notifiable disease in Karnataka since 2022.

### What about the dog?

- **Don't wait to see if the dog is healthy before starting the vaccine.** Start immediately.
- If the dog is a pet or a community dog you can observe, **watch it for 10 days.** Tell your doctor if it falls ill, changes behaviour, dies or disappears.
- If you know the dog's owner, ask for its rabies vaccination record.
- Report bites from community dogs to your local civic body, so the dog can be checked and vaccinated.

## Part 2: If your dog is bitten by another dog

Dog fights happen fast, and the damage is often worse than it looks. Canine teeth make small puncture wounds on the surface, but tear deeper tissue underneath.

### Immediate steps

1. **Keep yourself safe.** Never put your hands between fighting dogs. A frightened, injured dog may bite its own family — approach calmly and muzzle gently if needed.
2. **Check the whole body.** Part the fur and look for punctures, especially around the neck, legs, chest and belly.
3. **Control bleeding** with gentle, firm pressure using a clean cloth for a few minutes.
4. **Clean gently** with saline (or clean lukewarm water), then dilute povidone-iodine to the colour of weak tea.
5. **See a vet the same day.** Even small punctures often become infected or form abscesses within 2–3 days.

### What not to put on your dog's wound

- **Human antibiotic and steroid creams** — dogs lick them off, and some ingredients aren't safe to swallow
- **Zinc oxide creams** — harmful if licked
- **Hydrogen peroxide** repeatedly — it damages healing tissue
- **Turmeric, chilli, oil or home remedies**

Your vet may prescribe oral antibiotics, pain relief and a cone to stop licking.

### Rabies and your dog

If your dog was bitten by an unknown or unvaccinated dog, tell your vet. A vaccinated dog usually needs a **rabies booster** after a bite. Keep your dog's rabies vaccination up to date every year — see our [puppy vaccination schedule](/puppy-vaccination-schedule-india-bangalore/).

### Emergency signs — go to a 24/7 vet now

- Bleeding that doesn't stop with 5 minutes of pressure
- Wounds on the chest or belly, or difficulty breathing
- Limping badly or not putting weight on a leg
- Pale gums, weakness or collapse
- A small dog bitten by a large dog — "big dog, little dog" injuries are often internal

## How to prevent dog bites

- Never approach an unfamiliar dog, or one that is eating, sleeping or with puppies.
- Teach children not to hug, chase, tease or stare at dogs, and never to run from one.
- If a dog approaches aggressively, stand still, avoid eye contact, and back away slowly. Most dogs don't bite without provocation.
- Keep your own dog vaccinated, socialised and leashed in public.
- Learn the warning signs: stiff body, raised hackles, lip curling, growling, a hard stare.
- Support vaccination and sterilisation of community dogs — it's the most effective long-term way to cut rabies.
