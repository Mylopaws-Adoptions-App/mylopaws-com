## Questions to ask before you book

1. **Which rooms allow pets?** Often only certain cottages do.
2. **Is there a pet fee or deposit?** Get the amount in writing.
3. **Where can my dog go?** Restaurant, pool area and lawns often have different rules.
4. **Are there other animals on site?** Farm animals, resident dogs or cats matter if your dog is reactive.
5. **How far is the nearest vet?** Save the number before you leave.
6. **Is the property fenced?** Unfenced estates and plantations are easy to get lost in.

## What to pack

- Food and treats for every day, plus one extra day
- Bowls, bedding or a familiar blanket
- Leash, harness and a spare
- Medication, tick preventive and a basic first-aid kit
- Vaccination record (some properties ask to see it)
- Poop bags and a towel for muddy paws
