### 1. VAMA Retreats — Nandi Hills

**Area:** Oodanahalli / Acharalahalli, at the foothills of Nandi Hills · **From Bangalore:** about 52 km, roughly 45 minutes from the airport

VAMA Retreats is the stay Bangalore pet parents recommend most often. Spread across a large campus of vineyards, lakes, organic farm plots and lawns, it is built for families who travel with dogs: pets can run off-leash in safe areas, and there is a dedicated **dog pool**. The garden villas are the pet-friendly rooms to ask for.

- **Best for:** first trips, active dogs, families
- **Ask when booking:** which villa categories take pets, and the pet fee for your dates

## ✅ Pre-booking checklist

Ask every property these six questions before you pay. A place that hesitates on any of them is one to skip.

1. **Which rooms allow pets?** Often only certain cottages or villas do. "Pets allowed on the lawn" is not pet-friendly.
2. **What is the pet fee, and is there a deposit?** Get the amount in writing, per pet and per night.
3. **Where can my dog go?** Restaurant, pool, lawns and common areas often have different rules.
4. **Are there size or breed limits?** Hotels are stricter than farm stays and villas.
5. **Are there other animals on site?** Resident dogs, cats, poultry or cattle matter if your dog is reactive or has a high prey drive.
6. **Is the property fenced, and where is the nearest vet?** Save the vet's number before you leave Bangalore.

## 🧳 What to pack

- Food and treats for every day of the trip, plus one extra day (don't switch food on holiday)
- Bowls, bedding or a familiar blanket that smells of home
- Leash, harness and a spare
- Medication, tick and flea preventive, and a basic first-aid kit
- Vaccination record (many properties ask to see it)
- Poop bags, a towel for muddy paws, and an old sheet for the bed
