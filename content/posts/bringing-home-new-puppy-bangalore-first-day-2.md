The first day sets the tone for everything that follows. A little preparation makes it calmer for your puppy and for you.

## Before your puppy arrives

**Set up a puppy zone:** a quiet corner with a bed or crate, water, and a washable floor. Puppies feel safer in a small space at first than with the run of the house.

**Puppy-proof your home:**

- tuck away electrical wires and phone chargers
- move shoes, cushions and anything chewable out of reach
- block balconies, gaps in grilles and stairs
- check houseplants; several common ones are toxic to dogs

**Shopping list:**

- the same food your puppy eats now (switch slowly later, if at all)
- food and water bowls
- a soft bed or crate with a blanket
- a light collar with an ID tag, and a leash
- puppy pads or newspaper for toilet training
- a few safe chew toys
- a gentle puppy shampoo (for later; see [bathing your puppy](/bathing-puppy-bangalore-guide/))

**Find a vet** near you and book a first check-up within the first week.

## The ride home

Bring a carrier or have someone hold the puppy on a towel in the back seat. Expect some crying or drooling. A [pet-friendly cab](/travel-intracity/) helps if you do not have a car.

## The first day

1. **Show them the toilet spot first,** before anything else.
2. **Then let them explore the puppy zone,** calmly. No crowd of visitors on day one.
3. **Feed small meals** at the times they are used to.
4. **Let them sleep.** Puppies sleep 16 to 20 hours a day.
5. **Start gentle routines:** the same toilet spot after every meal, nap and play.

## The first night

Your puppy has just left its mother and siblings. Crying is normal.

- Put the bed or crate near you for the first nights.
- A warm (not hot) water bottle wrapped in a towel and a ticking clock can be comforting.
- Take them out to the toilet spot once or twice in the night.
- Stay calm and boring at night: no play.

## The first week

- vet check-up and a vaccination and deworming plan (see the [puppy vaccination schedule](/puppy-vaccination-schedule-india-bangalore/))
- start [potty training](/puppy-potty-training-bangalore-guide/)
- short, gentle handling of paws, ears and mouth every day
