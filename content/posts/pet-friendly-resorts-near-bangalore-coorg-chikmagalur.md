## First — the 5-question booking test

"Pet-friendly" still means different things at different properties. Before booking any resort, ask these five questions over WhatsApp or email. A property that hesitates on any of them is one to skip.

1. **Are dogs allowed inside the room — or only on the property?** "Pets allowed on the lawn" isn't pet-friendly. You want bed-and-bathroom access at minimum.
2. **What's the pet fee — per pet, per night, or per stay?** Many properties charge a pet fee; a few charge nothing. Know it upfront and get it in writing. *(Pet fees in this guide were correct when we checked; confirm current rates when you book.)*
3. **Are there breed or size restrictions?** Many hotels exclude Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, Dobermans and German Shepherds, or set a weight limit.
4. **Is the dining area dog-accessible?** The best properties let dogs sit with you at outdoor dining. Others bring meals to your room.
5. **What happens if my dog barks at night or sheds heavily?** A property used to dogs will laugh it off. One that hesitates may add a hefty "cleaning" charge at checkout.

Our [complete outstation travel checklist](/outstation-travel-pet-bangalore-checklist/) goes deep on prep. The short version:
