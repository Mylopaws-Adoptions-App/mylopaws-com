Neem has been part of Indian skin care for generations. For dogs, used properly, it is one of the most useful natural ingredients there is.

## What neem does

Neem is valued for being:

- **antibacterial and antifungal,** helpful for skin prone to infection
- **soothing,** calming itching and redness
- **a natural deterrent** for ticks, fleas and mosquitoes

That combination makes it well suited to Bangalore, where humidity, ticks and skin infections often appear together.

## Why neem oil at home is not the same

Pure neem oil is strong. Applied undiluted, it can irritate skin, especially sensitive skin. It tastes bitter and dogs lick it off, which can upset their stomachs. And it does not reach the skin well through a thick coat.

**Concentration, preparation and how it is worked into the skin all matter.** That is the difference between a neem treatment that helps and one that irritates.

## What neem does not do

Neem helps deter parasites, but it **does not replace** a vet-recommended tick and flea preventive. And it is not a treatment for serious infections, open wounds or allergies that need a vet.
