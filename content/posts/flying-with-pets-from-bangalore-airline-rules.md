Flying with a pet from Bangalore is easier than it was a few years ago. Air India and Akasa Air now take cats and dogs in the cabin, and Akasa alone has flown more than 10,000 pets since 2022 — with the Bengaluru–Delhi route among its busiest for pet travel.

But the rules differ by airline, the weight limits include the carrier, and one missing document can mean your pet is refused at check-in. This guide covers each airline's rules, what the numbers mean for common Indian breeds, and how to get your pet to Kempegowda International Airport without stress.

> **Airline rules change.** Everything below is taken from the airlines' own pages and recent reporting, as of November 2026. Always confirm with the airline when you book.

## Which airlines fly pets in India?

| | **Air India** | **Akasa Air** | **IndiGo** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pets in the cabin** | Yes — cats and dogs | Yes — cats and dogs | No |
| **Cabin weight limit** | 10 kg, pet + carrier | 10 kg | — |
| **Cabin carrier size** | 17 × 10 × 9 in (about 43 × 25 × 23 cm), soft-sided | 19 × 12.6 × 10 in (about 48 × 32 × 25 cm) | — |
| **Heavier pets** | 10–32 kg as checked baggage; over 32 kg as cargo | Up to 32 kg at airport check-in; up to 100 kg via cargo terminal | No pets in hold or cargo |
| **Minimum age** | 8 weeks | 3 months | — |
| **Book at least** | 48 hours before departure | 24 hours before departure | — |
| **Domestic fees** | Rs 7,500 cabin; Rs 16,000 checked | Rs 7,500 cabin; about Rs 15,000 hold | — |
| **Pets per flight** | Up to 2 in the Economy cabin | Up to 3 (2 in cabin, 1 in hold) | — |

**IndiGo** does not carry pets in the cabin, the hold or through its cargo service. The only exception is guide dogs accompanying visually impaired passengers. If you're flying with your pet, choose Air India or Akasa.

**Other airlines:** check directly with Air India Express and SpiceJet before you book — policies vary by route and aircraft.

## What the 10 kg cabin limit means for your dog

The cabin limit includes the carrier, and a soft carrier usually weighs 1–2 kg. So in practice, **your pet should weigh about 8 kg or less** to fly in the cabin.

| Dog | Typical adult weight in India | How it usually flies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chihuahua, Pomeranian | 2–4 kg | Cabin |
| Shih Tzu, Lhasa Apso | 4–8 kg | Cabin (Shih Tzus can't go in Air India's hold) |
| Pug | 6–9 kg | Cabin if under the limit — flat-faced, so ask your vet first |
| Beagle, Cocker Spaniel | 9–14 kg | Hold / checked baggage |
| Indie | 15–25 kg | Hold / checked baggage |
| Labrador, Golden Retriever | 25–36 kg | Hold — or cargo if dog + crate exceed 32 kg |
| German Shepherd, Rottweiler | 30–50 kg | Usually cargo |

**Cats** almost always fit within the cabin limit.

**Weigh your pet in its carrier** on a home scale before you book. Airlines weigh both at the counter.

## Air India's pet policy

Air India's "Paws on Board" policy allows **domesticated cats and dogs** on domestic and most international routes.

**In the cabin**

- Pet + carrier must weigh **10 kg or less**
- Soft, leak-proof, ventilated (on three sides) carrier up to **17 × 10 × 9 inches (about 43 × 25 × 23 cm)**, with no wheels, that fits under the seat
- **Economy class only**, in an aisle seat in the last five rows
- Up to **2 pets** in the Economy cabin per flight
- Pets must be at least **8 weeks old**
- **Domestic fee: Rs 7,500** per sector, generally non-refundable

**As checked baggage or cargo**

- **10–32 kg** (pet + crate): checked baggage in a hard crate. **Domestic fee: Rs 16,000**
- **Over 32 kg**: book as cargo
- **Snub-nosed breeds** — Pugs, Shih Tzus, Bulldogs and similar — are **not accepted in the hold**

**Air India won't accept**

- Pregnant or sedated pets
- Pets travelling with unaccompanied minors
- Pets with passengers using wheelchair assistance or travelling with an infant (in the cabin)

**Book at least 48 hours before departure.**

## Akasa Air's pet policy

Akasa Air's "Pets on Akasa" service carries **cats and dogs** on domestic flights within India.

- **Cabin:** up to **10 kg**, in a closed, zipped carrier up to **19 × 12.6 × 10 inches (about 48 × 32 × 25 cm)**
- **Airport check-in (hold):** up to **32 kg**, in a container up to **36 × 28 × 28 inches (about 91 × 71 × 71 cm)**
- **Cargo terminal:** pets over 32 kg, up to 100 kg
- **Up to 3 pets per flight** — 2 in the cabin and 1 in the hold
- Pets must be at least **3 months old**
- **Perks:** a complimentary pre-booked window seat, plus priority check-in and boarding
- **Fees (as reported in October 2026):** Rs 7,500 for the cabin, about Rs 15,000 for the hold

**How to book:** at least **24 hours before your flight**, through the Akasa Air Care Centre (+91 96061 12131) or pets@akasaair.com. Pets over 32 kg are booked at the cargo terminal.

## Documents you'll need

Requirements differ slightly by airline, so check your booking confirmation. Carry **originals and copies**.

| Document | Air India | Akasa Air |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vaccination record** (including rabies) | Up-to-date vaccination record / pet passport | Vaccination certificate from a registered vet |
| **Fit-to-fly health certificate** | Issued by a registered vet **within 7 days** of departure | Issued by a registered vet within **72 hours** of travel |
| **Indemnity form** | 2 copies (direct flights), 4 (connecting) | Download from Akasa's website |

**Tips:**

- Book the vet visit for the certificate **after** you confirm your flight dates, so it's within the airline's window.
- Make sure your dog's **rabies vaccination** is current — see our [puppy vaccination schedule](/puppy-vaccination-schedule-india-bangalore/).
- Keep a photo of every document on your phone as backup.

## Choosing and preparing the carrier

- **Buy the carrier at least two weeks before the flight.** Leave it open at home with a blanket and treats so your pet treats it as a den.
- **Check the size** against your airline's limit — measure it yourself; "airline approved" labels don't always match Indian airline sizes.
- **For the hold:** a hard, IATA-style crate with a secure door, ventilation on several sides, and room for your dog to stand, turn and lie down.
- **Line it** with an absorbent pad, and attach a water bowl that clips to the door.
- **Label it** with your name, phone number, flight details and "Live animal".

## Before the flight

- **Feed a light meal** about 4–6 hours before the flight, not right before.
- **A long walk** before leaving home helps your dog settle and toilet.
- **Don't sedate.** Air India refuses sedated pets, and sedatives can affect breathing and balance at altitude. Ask your vet about calming options instead.
- **Flat-faced breeds** (Pugs, Shih Tzus, Bulldogs, Persian cats) are more prone to breathing trouble — get a vet check before flying.
- **Avoid peak heat** if your pet is flying in the hold; early-morning or late-evening flights are cooler.

## Getting to Bangalore airport with your pet

Kempegowda International Airport is about **35–40 km** from central Bangalore — often 1 to 2 hours in traffic. Many app cabs refuse pets, and most can't fit an airline crate.

**Plan your airport trip:**

- **Leave extra time.** Pet check-in takes longer — originals are verified and your pet is weighed. Aim to reach the airport at least 2.5–3 hours before a domestic flight, or as your airline advises.
- **Go to the airline counter**, not a self check-in kiosk.
- **Keep your pet in its carrier** inside the terminal.
- **Carry water, poop bags and absorbent pads** for the wait.
