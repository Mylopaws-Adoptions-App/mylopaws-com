Ask most Bangalore pet parents what went wrong on a trip with their dog and you hear the same story. The appointment was booked, the bag was packed, and then the cab cancelled at the gate.

**The most common mistake is treating the ride as an afterthought.**

## Why the ride matters so much

- **Cancellations.** Many regular cab drivers refuse pets. You find out at the last minute, often after a 15-minute wait.
- **Stress before you arrive.** A dog who has waited, been refused and squeezed into a small car arrives at the vet or salon already anxious.
- **Unsafe set-ups.** Dogs in the front seat, heads out of windows, no harness: one sudden brake in Bangalore traffic is enough for an injury.
- **Heat.** A car waiting in the sun heats up fast. Dogs cannot cool down by sweating the way we do.

## How to get it right

1. **Book the ride when you book the appointment.** Not when you are ready to leave.
2. **Use a cab that expects your pet.** It removes the cancellation risk completely.
3. **Leave buffer time.** Bangalore traffic is unpredictable; a rushed pet parent means a stressed pet.
4. **Secure your pet** with a harness, crate or carrier.
5. **Bring water and a towel,** even for a short trip.
6. **Plan the return.** Getting home after a vet visit or grooming session is often harder to arrange than getting there.
