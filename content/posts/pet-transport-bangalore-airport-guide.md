## Flying your pet out of Bangalore? Here's how airport pet transport actually works.

Getting a pet to Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) is not like booking a normal cab. The timing is tighter, the vehicle matters more, and a stressed animal at 4 a.m. is the last thing you want before a flight. This guide walks Bangalore pet parents through the whole journey, and it matters, because India's **pet services market was worth USD 1.95 billion in 2024 and is growing at nearly 11% a year** (Grand View Research), with pet travel one of the fastest-rising needs as **70% of India's pet parents in 2024 were first-time owners** (Expert Market Research) doing this for the very first time.

## Start with the airline, not the cab

Before anything else, confirm your airline's live-animal policy. Rules differ by carrier and change often, so check directly for:

- **Cabin vs cargo** eligibility and weight limits
- Approved **crate dimensions and type**
- **Health certificate** and vaccination requirements (and how recent they must be)
- Advance booking windows, pet slots on a flight are limited

Get this locked first; everything else is built around it.

## The documents to carry

For domestic pet travel from Bangalore, keep a folder with:

- Up-to-date **vaccination records** (especially rabies)
- A **veterinary health/fitness certificate**, dated as your airline requires
- Any airline-specific pet forms
- Your booking confirmation for the pet

Carry originals plus a photo of each on your phone.

## Timing: build in a calm buffer

Airports mean queues, and a rushed animal is an anxious animal. Plan to reach BLR with a comfortable buffer over the airline's stated pet check-in time. That buffer is exactly why the *vehicle* matters, a cab that refuses your pet, or a driver who's visibly uneasy, can wreck the timing and the mood before you've even checked in.

## Why the cab is the part people underestimate

## A simple airport-day checklist

1. Confirm airline pet policy and book the pet's slot early.
2. Get the vet health certificate dated within the airline's window.
3. Pack the document folder (originals + phone photos).
4. Prep the crate, familiar bedding, a favourite item.
5. Light meal timing to avoid an upset stomach in transit.
6. Book a pet-ready cab with a calm buffer, not a last-minute ride-hail.
7. Toilet break before you leave and again at the airport if time allows.
