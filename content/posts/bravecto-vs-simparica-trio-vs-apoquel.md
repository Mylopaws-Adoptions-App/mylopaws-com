## Why Bangalore dogs need tick protection year-round

Ticks in Bangalore aren't just a monsoon problem. The brown dog tick (*Rhipicephalus sanguineus*), India's most common dog tick, lives in grass, park edges, walls and even apartment corridors, and it spreads **tick fever** — ehrlichiosis, babesiosis, hepatozoonosis and anaplasmosis.

A 2011 multi-region Indian study of 525 dogs found *Hepatozoon* in 30% and *Ehrlichia* in about 21% of dogs, and many carried more than one infection. Read our [tick season guide](/tick-season-bangalore-protect-your-dog/) for Bangalore-specific advice.

**Signs of tick fever:** fever, tiredness, loss of appetite, pale gums, nosebleeds, bruising or dark urine. See a vet promptly — it's treatable when caught early.

## Bravecto for dogs

Bravecto is a chewable tablet containing **fluralaner**, a member of the isoxazoline family. After your dog eats it, the drug circulates in the blood. When a tick or flea bites, it takes in the drug and dies.

### What it covers

- **Fleas:** protects for 12 weeks, killing fleas within about 8 hours of them biting
- **Ticks:** protects against most ticks for 12 weeks, killing attached ticks within about 12 hours
- **Mites:** treats *Demodex* mange, sarcoptic mange and ear mites
- **Flea allergy:** can be part of a plan to control flea allergy dermatitis

### Bravecto packs sold in India

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bravecto 2–4.5 kg** | 112.5 mg | 2 – 4.5 kg | Pomeranian, Chihuahua, toy breeds |
| **Bravecto 4.5–10 kg** | 250 mg | over 4.5 – 10 kg | Shih Tzu, Lhasa Apso, Pug, Dachshund |
| **Bravecto 10–20 kg** | 500 mg | over 10 – 20 kg | Beagle, Cocker Spaniel, smaller Indies |
| **Bravecto 20–40 kg** | 1000 mg | over 20 – 40 kg | Labrador, Golden Retriever, German Shepherd, larger Indies |
| **Bravecto 40–56 kg** | 1400 mg | over 40 – 56 kg | Rottweiler, Great Dane, other giant breeds |

- **One tablet per dose**, chosen by your dog's weight — e.g. a 15 kg Indie takes one 500 mg tablet.
- **Give it with a meal** — food improves absorption.
- **Weigh your dog** before each dose; puppies change bands quickly.
- **Don't break or split** tablets.
- Not for puppies under 8 weeks of age or dogs under 2 kg.

### Who it suits

- Dogs that need only tick and flea control
- Pet parents who want **fewer doses** — about four a year instead of twelve

### Side effects

The most common are mild and short-lived: reduced appetite, drooling, diarrhoea or vomiting. Very rarely, dogs show tiredness, itching, tremors, wobbliness or seizures. **Use with caution in dogs with epilepsy**, and tell your vet about any seizure history. Ask your vet before using it in pregnant, nursing or breeding dogs.

## Simparica Trio

Simparica Trio is a monthly chewable that combines three medicines:

- **Sarolaner** — an isoxazoline that kills ticks and fleas
- **Moxidectin** — prevents heartworm
- **Pyrantel** — treats roundworms and hookworms

### What it covers

- **Ticks and fleas**, one dose every month; it starts killing fleas within hours
- **Heartworm prevention** (*Dirofilaria immitis*, spread by mosquitoes)
- **Roundworms and hookworms**, including *Toxocara* and *Ancylostoma* — two worms that can also infect people
- **Mange:** *Demodex* and sarcoptic mange

### Simparica Trio packs sold in India

Simparica Trio is sold in India in five weight bands, usually in packs of 3 tablets (a three-month supply). The smallest pack is for dogs from **2.5 kg**.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Simparica Trio 2.5–5 kg** | 6 mg / 0.12 mg / 25 mg | 2.5 – 5 kg | Pomeranian, Chihuahua, toy breeds |
| **Simparica Trio 5–10 kg** | 12 mg / 0.24 mg / 50 mg | over 5 – 10 kg | Shih Tzu, Lhasa Apso, Pug, Dachshund |
| **Simparica Trio 10–20 kg** | 24 mg / 0.48 mg / 100 mg | over 10 – 20 kg | Beagle, Cocker Spaniel, smaller Indies |
| **Simparica Trio 20–40 kg** | 48 mg / 0.96 mg / 200 mg | over 20 – 40 kg | Labrador, Golden Retriever, German Shepherd, larger Indies |
| **Simparica Trio 40–60 kg** | 72 mg / 1.44 mg / 300 mg | over 40 – 60 kg | Rottweiler, Great Dane, other giant breeds |

- **One tablet a month**, chosen by your dog's weight — e.g. a 30 kg Labrador takes one 20–40 kg tablet each month.
- Not for puppies under **8 weeks of age** or dogs under **2.5 kg**.
- **Weigh your dog** before each dose and don't split tablets.
- **Test for heartworm first.** Simparica Trio prevents heartworm but does not kill adult worms already present.

### Who it suits

- Dogs that need **ticks, fleas, heartworm and worms covered in one monthly tablet**
- Dogs that scavenge, eat soil or go to busy parks, where worm risk is high
- Pet parents who prefer a simple monthly routine

### Who should avoid it

- **Pregnant, nursing or breeding dogs** — safety hasn't been established
- **Dogs with the MDR1 mutation (for example, Collies):** use only under veterinary guidance, with strict weight-based dosing

### Side effects

Rare, and usually mild and passing: vomiting, diarrhoea, tiredness, loss of appetite, or — very rarely — tremors, wobbliness or seizures.

## Bravecto vs Simparica Trio: which to choose?

| If your dog… | Better fit |
| --- | --- |
| Needs only tick and flea control | **Bravecto** |
| Needs heartworm prevention and deworming too | **Simparica Trio** |
| Weighs between 2 and 2.5 kg | **Bravecto** — the smallest Simparica Trio pack in India starts at 2.5 kg |
| Is pregnant, nursing or used for breeding | Talk to your vet |
| Has a history of seizures | Talk to your vet — both are isoxazolines |
| Has the MDR1 mutation, or is a Collie-type breed | Talk to your vet before Simparica Trio |
| You keep forgetting monthly doses | **Bravecto** |

**Both belong to the isoxazoline family.** The US FDA has advised that isoxazoline products can, rarely, cause tremors, wobbliness or seizures, even in dogs with no history of them. The FDA still considers them safe and effective for most dogs. Watch your dog for a day or two after the first dose.

**What about NexGard Spectra?** It's another monthly combination (afoxolaner + milbemycin oxime) covering ticks, fleas, heartworm and some intestinal worms — a close alternative to Simparica Trio. Your vet may recommend it instead.

## Apoquel: for allergic itching, not ticks

Apoquel (oclacitinib) doesn't kill a single tick or flea. It works by blocking the signals inside the body that drive allergic itching and inflammation. That's why it's often used **alongside** a tick and flea tablet — parasites trigger itching, and itchy dogs need both problems handled.

### What it treats

- **Itching from allergic skin disease**, including flea allergy, food allergy and environmental allergies
- **Atopic dermatitis** — long-term allergic skin disease

### How it's dosed

The standard dose on the label, and the one vets follow, is **0.4–0.6 mg per kg of body weight, twice a day for up to 14 days, then once a day** to keep itching under control. It can be given with or without food.

**What that looks like in India:** Apoquel is commonly sold here as **5.4 mg and 16 mg tablets**, usually in boxes of 20 (two strips of 10). Using the manufacturer's dosing chart with these strengths:

| Dog's weight | Tablets per dose (Indian strengths) | Typical adult dogs in India |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.5 – 5.9 kg | ½ × 5.4 mg | Small Shih Tzu, Lhasa Apso |
| 9 – 13.4 kg | 1 × 5.4 mg | Beagle, Cocker Spaniel |
| 13.5 – 19.9 kg | ½ × 16 mg | Smaller Indies |
| 20 – 26.9 kg | 2 × 5.4 mg | Larger Indies, smaller Labradors |
| 27 – 39.9 kg | 1 × 16 mg | Labrador, Golden Retriever, German Shepherd |
| 40 – 54.9 kg | 1½ × 16 mg | Rottweiler, large German Shepherd |
| 55 – 80 kg | 2 × 16 mg | Great Dane, other giant breeds |

For dogs under 4.5 kg, or between 6 and 9 kg, the manufacturer's chart uses a **3.6 mg** tablet, which is not commonly stocked in India — your vet will advise the right dose.

**Example:** a 10 kg Beagle needs 4–6 mg per dose, so one 5.4 mg tablet twice a day for up to two weeks, then once a day.

### Who should not take Apoquel

- **Dogs under 12 months of age**
- Dogs with **serious infections** or a weakened immune system
- Dogs with **cancer**, or a history of it (weigh the risks with your vet)
- **Breeding, pregnant or nursing dogs**

### Side effects and monitoring

In trials, the most common side effects were mild: diarrhoea, vomiting, low appetite, tiredness and drinking more. Apoquel can make dogs more prone to **infections**, including skin and ear infections and *Demodex* mange. For long-term use, your vet will recommend **regular blood tests**.

### Apoquel treats the itch, not the cause

Apoquel brings fast relief, but itching always has a reason. Your vet will look for:

- **Fleas and ticks** — handle these first; see above
- **Food allergy** — confirmed only by a strict 8-week elimination diet. Ask your vet about hydrolysed or novel-protein diets
- **Environmental allergies** — dust mites, pollen, moulds
- **Skin infections** — bacterial or yeast, very common in Bangalore's humidity

**Alternatives your vet may discuss:** steroids for short flare-ups, and lokivetmab (Cytopoint), a monthly injection for allergic itching. Ask whether it's available locally.

## Which one does your dog need?

| What you see | What usually helps |
| --- | --- |
| Ticks on your dog, or you want to prevent them | **Bravecto** or **Simparica Trio** |
| Fleas, flea dirt, or scratching around the tail base | **Bravecto** or **Simparica Trio**, plus treating your home |
| You also want heartworm prevention and deworming | **Simparica Trio** (or NexGard Spectra) |
| Constant itching, licking paws, red skin, recurring ear infections | A vet visit — possibly **Apoquel**, after parasites and infections are ruled out |
| Hair loss with crusts or intense itching | A vet visit — could be **mange**, which Bravecto and Simparica Trio both treat |

**Never use dog products on cats.** Some dog spot-ons contain permethrin, which is highly toxic to cats. Use only products made and dosed for cats.
