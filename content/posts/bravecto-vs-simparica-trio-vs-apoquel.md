Search "Bravecto vs Simparica vs Apoquel" and you'd think they were three versions of the same thing. They're not. **Bravecto and Simparica Trio stop ticks and fleas. Apoquel stops itching.** Many Bangalore dogs end up needing one of the first two — and some need Apoquel as well.

## Why Bangalore dogs need tick protection year-round

Ticks in Bangalore aren't just a monsoon problem. The brown dog tick (*Rhipicephalus sanguineus*), India's most common dog tick, lives in grass, park edges, walls and even apartment corridors, and it spreads **tick fever** — ehrlichiosis, babesiosis, hepatozoonosis and anaplasmosis.

A study of 525 dogs across four Indian regions found *Hepatozoon* in 30% and *Ehrlichia* in about 21% of dogs, and many carried more than one infection. Read our [tick season guide](/tick-season-bangalore-protect-your-dog/) for Bangalore-specific advice.

**Signs of tick fever:** fever, tiredness, loss of appetite, pale gums, nosebleeds, bruising or dark urine. See a vet promptly — it's treatable when caught early.

## Bravecto for dogs

Bravecto is a chewable tablet containing **fluralaner**, a member of the isoxazoline family. After your dog eats it, the drug circulates in the blood. When a tick or flea bites, it takes in the drug and dies.

### What it covers

- **Fleas:** kills new fleas for 12 weeks, starting within 8 hours of the flea biting
- **Ticks:** 12 weeks for most tick species. The European label lists **8 weeks for the brown dog tick** — the main tick in India — so ask your vet whether to redose earlier in peak tick season
- **Mange:** a single dose treats demodex and sarcoptic mange
- **Flea allergy:** can be part of a plan to control flea allergy dermatitis

### Bravecto dosage by weight

| Tablet | Dog's weight |
| --- | --- |
| **112.5 mg** | 2 – 4.5 kg |
| **250 mg** | over 4.5 – 10 kg |
| **500 mg** | over 10 – 20 kg |
| **1000 mg** | over 20 – 40 kg |
| **1400 mg** | over 40 – 56 kg |

- **Give it with a meal** — food improves absorption.
- **Weigh your dog** before each dose; puppies change bands quickly.
- **Don't break or split** tablets.
- Not for puppies under 8 weeks or dogs under 2 kg.

### Who it suits

- Dogs that need only tick and flea control
- Pet parents who want **fewer doses** — four a year instead of twelve
- **Breeding, pregnant and nursing dogs**, where its European label confirms safety

### Side effects

The most common are mild and short-lived: reduced appetite, drooling, diarrhoea or vomiting (between 1 and 10 in 100 dogs). Very rarely — fewer than 1 in 10,000 — dogs show tiredness, itching, tremors, wobbliness or seizures. **Use with caution in dogs with epilepsy**, and tell your vet about any seizure history.

## Simparica Trio

Simparica Trio is a monthly chewable that combines three medicines:

- **Sarolaner** — an isoxazoline that kills ticks and fleas
- **Moxidectin** — prevents heartworm and lungworm
- **Pyrantel** — treats roundworms and hookworms

### What it covers

- **Ticks and fleas** for at least 5 weeks after each dose (given monthly). Existing fleas die within 8 hours; ticks within 48 hours
- **Heartworm prevention** (*Dirofilaria immitis*, spread by mosquitoes)
- **Roundworms and hookworms**, including *Toxocara* and *Ancylostoma* — two worms that can also infect people
- **Mange:** demodex and sarcoptic mange
- **Lungworm and eyeworm** prevention, and reduced tapeworm spread via fleas

### Simparica Trio dosage by weight

Simparica Trio is sold in India in weight bands of **2.5–5 kg, 5–10 kg, 10–20 kg, 20–40 kg and 40–60 kg**. A smaller 1.25–2.5 kg strength exists in some markets.

- Give **once a month**, at least a month apart.
- **Weigh your dog** before each dose and don't split tablets.
- **Test for heartworm first.** Simparica Trio prevents heartworm but does not kill adult worms already present.

### Who it suits

- Dogs that need **ticks, fleas, heartworm and worms covered in one monthly tablet**
- Dogs that scavenge, eat soil or go to busy parks, where worm risk is high
- Pet parents who prefer a simple monthly routine

### Who should avoid it

- **Pregnant, nursing or breeding dogs** — safety hasn't been established
- **Collies and related breeds** carrying the MDR1 gene mutation are more sensitive to moxidectin; the dose must be exact

### Side effects

Very rare — fewer than 1 in 10,000 dogs — and usually mild and passing: vomiting, diarrhoea, tiredness, loss of appetite, or tremors, wobbliness or seizures.

## Bravecto vs Simparica Trio: which to choose?

| If your dog… | Better fit |
| --- | --- |
| Needs only tick and flea control | **Bravecto** |
| Needs heartworm prevention and deworming too | **Simparica Trio** |
| Is pregnant, nursing or used for breeding | **Bravecto** |
| Has a history of seizures | Talk to your vet — both are isoxazolines |
| Is a Collie, Sheltie or related breed | Talk to your vet — check MDR1 status |
| You keep forgetting monthly doses | **Bravecto** |

**Both belong to the isoxazoline family.** The US FDA has advised that isoxazoline products can, rarely, cause tremors, wobbliness or seizures, even in dogs with no history of them. The FDA still considers them safe and effective for most dogs. Watch your dog for a day or two after the first dose.

**What about NexGard Spectra?** It's another monthly combination (afoxolaner + milbemycin oxime) covering ticks, fleas, heartworm and some intestinal worms — a close alternative to Simparica Trio. Your vet may recommend it instead.

## Apoquel: for itching, not ticks

Apoquel (oclacitinib) doesn't kill a single tick or flea. It works by blocking the signals inside the body that drive allergic itch and inflammation. That's why it's often used **alongside** a tick and flea tablet — parasites trigger itching, and itchy dogs need both problems handled.

### What it treats

- **Itching from allergic skin disease**, including flea allergy, food allergy and environmental allergies
- **Atopic dermatitis** — long-term allergic skin disease

### How it's given

- **0.4–0.6 mg per kg** of body weight, **twice a day for up to 14 days**, then **once a day** to keep itching under control
- Available in India as **3.6 mg, 5.4 mg and 16 mg** tablets
- Can be given with or without food
- Works quickly — it reaches peak levels in the blood in under an hour, and most dogs scratch noticeably less within days

### Who should not take Apoquel

- **Dogs under 12 months old**
- Dogs with **serious infections** or a weakened immune system
- Dogs with **cancer**, or a history of it (weigh the risks with your vet)
- **Breeding, pregnant or nursing dogs**

### Side effects and monitoring

In trials, the most common side effects were mild: diarrhoea, vomiting, low appetite, tiredness and drinking more. Apoquel can make dogs more prone to **infections**, including skin and ear infections and demodex mange. For long-term use, your vet will recommend **regular blood tests**.

### Apoquel treats the itch, not the cause

Apoquel brings fast relief, but itching always has a reason. Your vet will look for:

- **Fleas and ticks** — handle these first; see above
- **Food allergy** — confirmed only by a strict 8-week elimination diet. Ask your vet about hydrolysed or novel-protein diets
- **Environmental allergies** — dust mites, pollen, moulds
- **Skin infections** — bacterial or yeast, very common in Bangalore's humidity

**Alternatives your vet may discuss:** steroids for short flare-ups, and lokivetmab (Cytopoint), a monthly injection for allergic itch. Ask whether it's available locally.

## Which one does your dog need?

| What you see | What usually helps |
| --- | --- |
| Ticks on your dog, or you want to prevent them | **Bravecto** or **Simparica Trio** |
| Fleas, flea dirt, or scratching around the tail base | **Bravecto** or **Simparica Trio**, plus treating your home |
| You also want heartworm prevention and deworming | **Simparica Trio** (or NexGard Spectra) |
| Constant itching, licking paws, red skin, recurring ear infections | A vet visit — possibly **Apoquel**, after parasites and infections are ruled out |
| Hair loss with crusts or intense itch | A vet visit — could be **mange**, which Bravecto and Simparica Trio both treat |

**Never use dog products on cats.** Some dog spot-ons contain permethrin, which is highly toxic to cats. Use only products made and dosed for cats.
