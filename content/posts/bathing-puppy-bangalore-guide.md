A puppy's first baths shape how they feel about water and handling for life. Go slowly and keep it positive.

## When to start

Most puppies do not need a full bath in the first weeks at home. Wipe dirty patches with a damp cloth instead. Ask your vet when a full bath is fine for your puppy, especially if they are very young, small or unwell. For salon or doorstep grooming, wait until your vet confirms vaccinations are done.

## How often

For most puppies, **every three to four weeks** is plenty, unless they are visibly dirty. Bathing too often strips natural oils and causes dry, itchy skin.

## What you need

- a gentle puppy shampoo (never human shampoo or dish soap)
- lukewarm water
- a non-slip mat
- two towels
- treats

## Step by step

1. **Brush first** to remove loose fur and tangles.
2. **Check the water** with your wrist: lukewarm, never hot.
3. **Wet the body slowly,** from the back towards the head. Keep water out of ears and eyes.
4. **Lather gently** with a small amount of shampoo. Wash the face with a damp cloth only.
5. **Rinse thoroughly.** Leftover shampoo causes itching.
6. **Dry completely,** especially in Bangalore's humid months. Towel first, then a hair dryer on a cool, low setting if your puppy accepts it.
7. **Treats and praise** throughout.

## Making it calm

- Practise handling paws, ears and mouth every day, outside bath time.
- Keep the first baths short.
- Stop before your puppy is overwhelmed, and end on something good.
