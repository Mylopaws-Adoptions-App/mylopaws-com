A jacuzzi or hydro bath sounds like a luxury. For dogs, it is mostly about cleaning better.

## What it is

Instead of pouring water over the coat, a hydro bath circulates warm water with gentle bubbles and water movement through the fur, right down to the skin.

## Why it cleans better

- **It reaches the skin** through thick and double coats, where a jug of water does not.
- **It lifts dirt, dead hair and build-up** without hard scrubbing.
- **It helps shampoos and treatments work,** because they reach the skin evenly.
- **It loosens dead undercoat,** which makes de-shedding easier.

## Comfort and calm

Warm, moving water is soothing for many dogs, especially older dogs with stiff joints. Our team introduces it slowly, and nervous or first-time dogs are never forced.

## Who it suits

- double-coated breeds that shed heavily
- dogs with build-up, odour or dull fur
- older dogs who find scrubbing uncomfortable
- as part of therapies like [Fur Revival & Shine](/therapy-fur-revival-shine/), [De-Shedding](/therapy-de-shedding/) and [Deep Odour Control](/therapy-odour-control/)

## Who should wait

Dogs with open wounds, recent surgery, or certain heart or skin conditions. Tell us about your dog's health when you book, and we adjust the session.
