## How to choose a vet in Bangalore

1. **Distance matters most in an emergency.** Know your nearest 24/7 hospital, not just your favourite clinic.
2. **Check what's in-house.** X-ray, ultrasound, blood tests and surgery under one roof save precious time.
3. **Ask who's on duty at night.** At 24/7 hospitals, ask whether a senior vet is on call overnight.
4. **For skin problems, ask about dermatology experience.** Recurring itching needs diagnosis — skin scrapes, cytology, allergy work-up — not just repeated steroid shots.
5. **Keep records together.** Vaccination card, past prescriptions and test reports, in one folder and on your phone.

## What to do in a pet emergency

- **Call ahead** while you travel so the team is ready.
- **Keep your pet still and warm.** Carry small pets in a carrier; use a blanket as a stretcher for large dogs.
- **Swallowed something?** Bring the packaging or a photo. Don't make your pet vomit unless a vet tells you to.
- **Never give human medicines** — paracetamol and ibuprofen can be fatal to pets.
- **Heatstroke:** move to shade, cool with room-temperature (not ice-cold) water, and head to a vet.
- **Bleeding:** press firmly with a clean cloth and keep pressing on the way.

Signs you should go **now**: difficulty breathing, collapse, seizures, a swollen and hard belly, repeated vomiting or bloody diarrhoea, suspected poisoning, a male cat straining to urinate, or any major injury.
