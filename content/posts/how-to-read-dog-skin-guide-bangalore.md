Your dog's skin tells you a lot, if you know where to look. Do this quick check once a week.

## How to check

1. In good light, part the fur in several places: back, sides, belly, tail base.
2. Look inside and behind the ears.
3. Spread the toes and check between them and under the pads.
4. Lift the armpits and check skin folds.
5. Smell the skin and ears.

Healthy skin is pale pink or brown (or pigmented, depending on breed), smooth, with no strong smell.

## What the signs usually mean

| What you see | What it often points to |
| --- | --- |
| White flakes | Dry skin, harsh shampoo, diet |
| Redness with itching, no parasites | Allergy or irritation |
| Red patches with crusts, sour smell | Fungal or bacterial infection |
| Dark specks in fur | Flea dirt |
| Small bumps you can feel | Ticks, or insect bites |
| Moist, red, painful patch | Hot spot |
| Bald patches | Infection, mites, hormonal issues, over-licking |
| Greasy skin, strong smell | Oil imbalance, yeast |
| Dark, thickened skin | Long-term irritation |

This table is a guide, not a diagnosis.

## When to see a vet

- broken skin, bleeding or discharge
- sudden swelling or lumps that grow
- bald patches spreading quickly
- your dog seems unwell, off food or in pain
- itching that keeps your dog awake
