India has dozens of dog breeds to choose from — hardy native breeds bred for our climate, and imported breeds that have become household favourites. For years the Labrador, German Shepherd and Golden Retriever have dominated Indian homes, while the Siberian Husky has been rising fast — despite being one of the worst-suited breeds for Indian heat.

The right choice depends on your home, your time and Bangalore's weather. This guide covers the main types of dogs you'll meet in India, the smartest breeds, the low-shedding ones, and which suit city life.

## Indian dog breeds

Native breeds evolved in Indian heat. They're generally hardy, low-maintenance and have fewer inherited health problems than many imported breeds.

| Breed | Size | Known for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Indie (Indian pariah dog)** | Medium | Healthy, intelligent, alert; low grooming; superb family dogs |
| **Mudhol Hound** | Large | Karnataka's own sighthound; serves with the Indian Army |
| **Rajapalayam** | Large | Tamil Nadu sighthound; loyal and protective; serves with the Indian Army |
| **Chippiparai** | Medium–large | Slim, fast sighthound; reserved with strangers; serves with the Indian Army |
| **Kombai** | Medium–large | Muscular, fearless guardian from Tamil Nadu |
| **Kanni** | Medium–large | Gentle, shy sighthound |
| **Rampur Hound** | Large | North Indian sighthound; being trialled by the Army |
| **Himalayan Sheepdog / Gaddi** | Large | Mountain guardians — better suited to cooler regions |

The Indian Army's Remount and Veterinary Corps has inducted the Mudhol Hound, Chippiparai and Rajapalayam, and is trialling the Rampur Hound — a sign of how capable native breeds are.

**The Indie deserves special mention.** Bangalore's rescues always have Indie puppies and adults looking for homes. They're adaptable, intelligent and among the healthiest dogs you can bring home — and adopting one gives a dog a second chance.

## The smartest dog breeds

Psychologist Stanley Coren's widely cited ranking, based on how quickly breeds learn and obey new commands, puts these at the top:

1. Border Collie
2. Poodle
3. German Shepherd
4. Golden Retriever
5. Doberman Pinscher
6. Shetland Sheepdog
7. Labrador Retriever
8. Papillon
9. Rottweiler
10. Australian Cattle Dog

**A smart dog isn't an easy dog.** Highly intelligent, high-energy breeds need daily training and mental work. Without it, they get bored and destructive. Many Indies are just as quick to learn, even though native breeds weren't part of the ranking.

## Dogs that don't shed (much)

No dog is completely non-shedding or allergy-free, but these breeds shed very little:

- **Poodle** (toy, miniature and standard)
- **Bichon Frise**
- **Maltese**
- **Shih Tzu**
- **Lhasa Apso**
- **Yorkshire Terrier**
- **Miniature Schnauzer**
- **Havanese**

**The trade-off:** low-shedding fur keeps growing, so these breeds need brushing several times a week and a professional groom every 4–8 weeks to prevent painful mats. In Bangalore's monsoon, dense fur that stays damp can lead to skin infections — dry thoroughly after every walk.

**Allergies?** People react to proteins in saliva and skin flakes, not just fur. Spend time with the breed before committing.

## Breeds that struggle in Indian heat

These breeds were built for cold climates or have breathing limitations. They suffer in Indian summers and need air conditioning, limited exercise and constant care.

- **Siberian Husky and Alaskan Malamute** — thick double fur designed for snow
- **Saint Bernard, Newfoundland, Tibetan Mastiff** — giant, heavy-furred mountain breeds
- **Chow Chow** — dense fur, prone to overheating
- **Flat-faced breeds** — Pug, French Bulldog, English Bulldog, and to a lesser degree Boxer — prone to breathing problems and heatstroke

Bangalore is milder than most Indian cities, but its normal April maximum is about 34°C, and recent Aprils have run hotter. If your heart is set on one of these breeds, plan for air-conditioned rest, early-morning walks only, and close watch for heatstroke.

**Signs of heatstroke:** heavy panting, drooling, bright red gums, wobbling, vomiting or collapse. Move your dog somewhere cool, wet the fur with cool (not ice-cold) water, and go to a vet immediately.

## Choosing a breed for a Bangalore apartment

Ask yourself honestly:

- **How much time do you have?** High-energy breeds need 1.5–2 hours of exercise daily.
- **How much space?** Large dogs can live in flats, but need more outdoor time.
- **Are you home during the day?** Few dogs cope well alone for 9–10 hours.
- **How much grooming can you manage?** Long-furred breeds need regular brushing and professional grooming.
- **Noise:** Beagles, Lhasas and some terriers can be vocal — your neighbours will notice.

**Good apartment fits:** Indie, Beagle, Shih Tzu, Lhasa Apso, Cocker Spaniel, Poodle, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel.

**Possible with commitment:** Labrador, Golden Retriever, German Shepherd, Doberman.

## Your rights in a housing society

A 2014 Animal Welfare Board of India circular on pet dogs says:

- Housing societies **cannot ban pet dogs**, even with a majority vote or a change in bye-laws.
- Pets **cannot be barred from lifts**, and societies **cannot charge** for lift use.
- **Size limits** and **barking** are not valid grounds for a ban.
- Banning pets from society gardens is discouraged; fixed walking times are suggested instead.

**Your part:** keep your dog leashed in common areas, pick up after it, keep vaccinations current, and be considerate about noise. Good neighbours prevent most disputes.

## Licensing in Bangalore

Bengaluru's civic body has registered pet dogs in the past. In September 2026, the Greater Bengaluru Authority's draft rules proposed:

- **Annual licences** for dogs over three months old
- A **lower fee for Indian and mixed-breed dogs** than for pedigree breeds
- **Rabies vaccination certificates** from an authorised vet
- **Microchipping** at the owner's cost
- **Limits on the number of dogs** per home, linked to home size

The rules were in draft at the time of writing. Check the current requirements with your local corporation office.
