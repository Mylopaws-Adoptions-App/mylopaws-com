There is no tick-free season in Bangalore. Ticks are active all year, with noticeable rises in the warmer months and after the rains, when grass and shrubs are thick.

## Why ticks matter

Ticks are not just itchy. They can spread **tick fever** (diseases such as ehrlichiosis and babesiosis), which can make dogs seriously ill.

**See a vet promptly if your dog has had ticks and shows:**

- fever, dullness or unusual tiredness
- loss of appetite
- pale gums
- nosebleeds or bruising
- weakness or difficulty walking

## Where ticks hide on your dog

Run your fingers slowly over your dog after every walk, and check:

- inside and behind the ears
- between the toes and under the paw pads
- under the collar and around the neck
- in the armpits and groin
- around the tail and eyelids

## Where ticks hide in your home

The brown dog tick can complete its whole life cycle indoors. Check and clean:

- your dog's bedding (wash hot, weekly in tick season)
- sofas, cushions and the corners of rooms
- cracks in walls and floors, and behind furniture

## How to protect your dog

1. **Use a vet-recommended preventive all year:** spot-ons, chewables or collars. Do not stop in the cooler months.
2. **Avoid long grass** and shrubby areas on walks where possible.
3. **Check daily** in peak season and after every outing.
4. **Treat the home** at the same time as your dog.
5. **Keep up grooming.** A well-kept coat makes ticks easier to spot.
