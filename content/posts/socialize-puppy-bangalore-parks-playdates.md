Puppies are most open to new experiences in their first few months. What they meet calmly now, they are more likely to accept for life: people, traffic, other dogs, cars, grooming.

## Why it matters

Under-socialised puppies often grow into dogs who are fearful or reactive with strangers, other dogs, vehicles or handling. Good socialisation is the best way to prevent that.

## Before vaccinations are complete

You do not have to wait to start. You just have to be careful where.

- **Carry your puppy** to see busy streets, markets and traffic from your arms.
- **Invite friends over:** people of different ages, people wearing hats, helmets or sunglasses.
- **Meet healthy, vaccinated adult dogs** you know, at home.
- **Introduce sounds:** pressure cookers, doorbells, scooters, thunder (at low volume at first).
- **Practise handling** of paws, ears and mouth daily.
- **Short car rides** to fun places; see [preparing for a first cab ride](/prepare-dog-first-cab-ride-bangalore/).

Avoid parks, pavements and places used by unknown dogs until your vet confirms your puppy is protected (see the [vaccination schedule](/puppy-vaccination-schedule-india-bangalore/)).

## After vaccinations

- **Choose quiet times** at parks first, early mornings on weekdays.
- **Pick playmates carefully:** calm, friendly dogs of a similar size.
- **Keep sessions short** and end before your puppy is tired or overwhelmed.
- **Consider a puppy class** with a trainer who uses reward-based methods.

## Bangalore-specific tips

- **Community dogs:** keep your puppy on a leash and give unknown street dogs space.
- **Heat:** avoid walks in the middle of the day, especially in summer.
- **Ticks:** check your puppy after every outing in grass; see our [tick season guide](/tick-season-bangalore-protect-your-dog/).

## Watch your puppy's body language

Tail tucked, ears back, trying to hide or freeze: give them space and go slower. Socialisation should feel safe, never forced.
