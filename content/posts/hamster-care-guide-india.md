Small, curious and endlessly busy at night, hamsters are one of India's most popular first pets. They're often sold as "easy" pets for children — but a hamster that lives a full, healthy life needs more space, the right food and protection from heat than most pet shops suggest.

Here's everything you need to know before you bring one home.

## Types of hamsters you'll find in India

| Type | Size | Lifespan | Temperament | Live alone? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Syrian (Golden)** | 15–18 cm, 90–140 g | 2–3 years | Calm, friendly, easiest to handle | **Always alone** |
| **Campbell's dwarf** | 8–10 cm, 40–60 g | 2–3 years | Can be nippy until it trusts you | Same-sex pair possible if introduced young |
| **Winter White dwarf** | 8–10 cm, 30–50 g | 1–3 years | Gentle but quick | Same-sex pair possible if introduced young |
| **Roborovski (Robo)** | 4–5 cm, 20–25 g | ~3 years, up to 4 | Very fast — better to watch than hold | Best alone, or in a very large enclosure |
| **Chinese** | 10–12 cm, 40–45 g | 2.5–3 years | Shy at first, then easy to handle | Usually alone |

**Best for beginners and families:** the Syrian. It's the largest, calmest and easiest to hold.

**Best to watch:** the Roborovski. The smallest and fastest hamster — fascinating to observe, but tiny enough to slip through small fingers.

**Watch out for mis-sexing.** Pet shops often get it wrong, and "one pet" can become a litter within weeks. Ask a vet to check, and never house males and females together.

## Where to get a hamster

- **Choose a responsible breeder or a rescue** over crowded shops and markets, where hamsters are often stressed, mis-sexed or already ill.
- **Pick an alert, active hamster** with clean eyes, nose and bottom, and a smooth, full fur.
- **Avoid** any hamster with a wet or dirty tail area, crusty eyes, sneezing, or hunched posture.
- **Bring it home in a ventilated box,** avoiding the hottest part of the day.

## Setting up a proper home

Most cages sold in India are far too small. Hamsters in cramped cages often develop stress behaviours like constant bar-biting, pacing and climbing.

| What | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| **Floor space** | At least **100 × 50 cm** for one hamster — bigger is better |
| **Bedding depth** | About **25 cm** of paper-based bedding for burrowing |
| **Wheel** | Solid surface, no rungs. At least **30 cm** across for Syrians, **20 cm** for dwarfs |
| **Hides** | At least one multi-chamber hide, plus tunnels |
| **Sand bath** | A dish of plain children's play sand or chinchilla sand (not dust) |
| **Chews** | Untreated wood, hay cubes — teeth grow throughout life |
| **Water** | A bottle or heavy, shallow bowl, refreshed daily |

A large glass tank, a big DIY bin cage or a wide wooden enclosure works far better than a small wire cage.

### Bedding to avoid

- **Pine and cedar shavings** — dusty, and can cause breathing problems
- **Fluffy "cotton" or kapok nesting material** — wraps around legs and can block the gut
- **Scented bedding** — irritates airways
- **Newspaper** — the ink can be harmful
- **Thick toilet paper** — can cause gut blockages

## What do hamsters eat?

### The base diet

- A **good-quality hamster pellet or lab block** gives balanced nutrition. Seed mixes alone let hamsters pick out the fatty favourites and leave the rest.
- Feed a measured amount once a day, in the evening when your hamster wakes.
- Scatter some food around the enclosure — foraging keeps hamsters busy and active.

### Fresh foods (small amounts, a few times a week)

- **Vegetables:** cucumber, carrot, broccoli, cauliflower, spinach, capsicum, peas
- **Fruit (occasional treat only):** apple without seeds, banana, pear, berries — fruit is sugary, and dwarf hamsters are prone to diabetes
- **Protein:** plain boiled egg, plain cooked chicken, mealworms

Remove uneaten fresh food the next day so it doesn't rot in the hoard.

### Never feed

- **Citrus fruits** — orange, lemon, mosambi
- **Onion, garlic, leeks**
- **Raw beans and raw potato**
- **Almonds** and apple seeds
- **Chocolate, sweets, salty or spicy food**
- **Sticky foods** like clumps of peanut butter — they can get stuck in cheek pouches

## How long do hamsters live?

Most pet hamsters live **2 to 3 years**. Roborovskis often reach around 3 years, and sometimes 4. Good food, a large enclosure, a stable temperature and early vet care for illness all help your hamster reach the upper end.

Because their lives are short, hamsters age fast. From around 18 months, watch for weight loss, thinning fur, lumps and slowing down, and take your hamster for a check-up.

## Hamsters and Indian summers

**Heat is one of the biggest dangers for hamsters in India.**

- Hamsters do best at about **18–25°C**. Long spells above about **25°C** raise the risk of heatstroke — and many Indian homes are warmer than that in summer.
- Bangalore is milder than most Indian cities, but its normal April maximum is about 34°C, and top-floor flats get much hotter indoors.

**Keeping your hamster cool:**

- Keep the enclosure **out of direct sun**, away from windows and the kitchen, in the **coolest room** in the house.
- Add a **ceramic tile, granite slab or ceramic hide** to lie on. On hot days, chill it in the fridge for a few minutes first.
- Offer a little cucumber for extra water — not too much, or it causes diarrhoea.
- Change water more often and add a second bottle or bowl. **Never add ice** or put the hamster in front of a fan blowing directly at it.
- Improve air flow around the enclosure — glass tanks need a mesh lid.

**Signs of heatstroke:** lying flat and stretched out, heavy breathing, wetness around the mouth, weakness or unresponsiveness. Move your hamster somewhere cool and call a vet immediately.

## Handling and daily life

- Hamsters are **nocturnal** — mostly active at dusk and through the night. Don't wake a sleeping hamster to play.
- Let a new hamster settle for a few days before handling.
- Start by letting it walk onto your open hand for a treat. Scoop from below — never grab from above, which feels like a predator.
- Handle low over a soft surface. A fall from standing height can badly injure a hamster.
- Children should always handle hamsters sitting down, with an adult nearby.
- **Spot-clean** soiled bedding every few days, but avoid full clean-outs more than every few weeks — they destroy burrows and stress your hamster.

## Health problems to watch for

| Problem | Signs | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wet tail** | Wet, dirty tail area, diarrhoea, hunched posture, lethargy | Emergency — see a vet the same day, especially in young hamsters |
| **Overgrown teeth** | Drooling, not eating, weight loss | Vet trims teeth; provide chews |
| **Heatstroke** | Lying flat, panting, weakness | Cool gently, call a vet |
| **Skin problems and mites** | Hair loss, scratching, flaky skin | Vet check |
| **Cheek pouch problems** | Swollen cheek that doesn't empty | Vet check |
| **Lumps and swellings** | New bumps, especially in older hamsters | Vet check |
| **Diabetes** (dwarf hamsters) | Drinking and peeing a lot | Vet check; cut sugary foods |
