## Shop orders

- **Before dispatch:** you can cancel a paid order by messaging us on WhatsApp. We refund the full amount to your original payment method automatically.
- **After dispatch:** orders cannot be cancelled once they are out for delivery. If you refuse a delivery, the delivery fee is not refunded.
- **Wrong, damaged or expired items:** tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo. We replace the item or refund it in full.
- **Medicines and health products:** for your pet's safety, medicines, supplements, medicated shampoos and anything opened or used cannot be returned once delivered, unless they are wrong, damaged, expired or defective.
- **Food, treats, hygiene and grooming products:** unopened and sealed products can be returned within 48 hours of delivery if you have changed your mind. We collect them, and the delivery fee is not refunded. Opened food is not returned, but if your pet has a reaction or the product is faulty, tell us and we will make it right.
- **Prescription items:** these are dispensed against your vet's prescription and cannot be returned once delivered, unless wrong, damaged or expired.
- **How to ask:** message the Homes & Shopping WhatsApp line (+91 90710 91699) with your order number and a photo. We reply within one working day.
- **If we cannot fulfil an order** (for example, an item cannot be sourced or a prescription is invalid), we tell you on WhatsApp, cancel that item or the order, and refund you in full.

## Travel, Dermacare and Homes bookings

- Please cancel or reschedule as early as possible, on WhatsApp or in the app. Any cancellation terms specific to your booking are confirmed with you when you book.
- If we cancel or cannot provide a confirmed service, you receive a full refund of anything you paid for it.
- If a service is stopped for your pet's safety (see our [Terms](/terms-of-service/)), we refund the part of the service not provided.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go to your original payment method through Razorpay. We start approved refunds within 2 working days. Banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show them.

## Not happy with how we handled it?

Write to our Grievance Officer at [customercare@mylopaws.com](mailto:customercare@mylopaws.com). We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within one month.
