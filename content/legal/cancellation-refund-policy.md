## Shop and pharmacy orders

- **Before dispatch:** you can cancel a paid order by messaging us on WhatsApp. We refund the full amount to your original payment method automatically.
- **After dispatch:** orders cannot be cancelled once they are out for delivery. If you refuse a delivery, the delivery fee is not refunded.
- **Wrong, damaged or expired items:** tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo. We replace the item or refund it in full.
- **Medicines:** for safety, medicines cannot be returned once delivered, unless they are wrong, damaged or expired.
- **If we cannot fulfil an order** (for example, an item is out of stock or a prescription is invalid), we cancel it and refund you in full.

## Travel, Dermacare and Homes bookings

- Please cancel or reschedule as early as possible, on WhatsApp or in the app. Any cancellation terms specific to your booking are confirmed with you when you book.
- If we cancel or cannot provide a confirmed service, you receive a full refund of anything you paid for it.
- If a service is stopped for your pet's safety (see our [Terms](/terms-of-service/)), we refund the part of the service not provided.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go to your original payment method through Razorpay. Once we start a refund, banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show it.
