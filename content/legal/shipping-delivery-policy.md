## Where we deliver

We deliver within Bengaluru. We do not ship outside the city at present.

## When

- We run one delivery round each evening, **Monday to Saturday, between 5 pm and 8 pm**.
- Orders paid **before 1 pm** go out the same evening. Orders after 1 pm, or on Sunday, go out on the next delivery day.
- Your expected delivery date is shown when you order and confirmed on WhatsApp.
- We stock the most popular items. If something is not on our shelf, we source it for you, so it can take an extra day or two. We tell you on WhatsApp if your delivery date changes.

## Delivery fee

- **Free delivery** when your products total **₹3,000 or more**.
- A flat **₹100** fee when your products total less than ₹3,000.
- The delivery fee is shown at checkout before you pay. Prices on the shop are in Indian rupees and include GST.

## Delivery area

We deliver to Bengaluru pincodes (560xxx). If your pincode is outside Bengaluru, checkout will not accept it.

## Order updates

We update you on WhatsApp on the number you gave at checkout: order confirmed, out for delivery and delivered. You can also check your order on the [order page](/shopping/order/) with your order number and phone number.

## Delivery attempts

Please be available at the address in the delivery window. If we cannot reach you, we will try once more the same evening or on the next delivery day. After that we will contact you to rearrange, and the delivery fee may apply again.

## Free store pickup

You can collect your order free of charge from our store in HSR Layout (764/A, 22nd Cross, Parangipalya Main Road, 2nd Sector, HSR Layout, Bengaluru 560102). We message you when it is ready.

## Prescription medicines

Prescription-only items are dispatched only after we have checked your vet's prescription.

## Problems with a delivery

If your order is late, damaged or wrong, message us on WhatsApp. See our [Cancellation & Refund Policy](/cancellation-refund-policy/).
