## Where we deliver

We deliver within Bengaluru. We do not ship outside the city at present.

## When

- We run one delivery round each evening, **Monday to Saturday, between 5 pm and 8 pm**.
- Orders paid **before 1 pm** go out the same evening. Orders after 1 pm, or on Sunday, go out on the next delivery day.
- Your expected delivery date is shown when you order and confirmed on WhatsApp.

## Delivery fee

- **Free delivery** on orders of ₹2,000 or more.
- A flat **₹100** fee on orders below ₹2,000.

## Free store pickup

You can collect your order free of charge from our store in HSR Layout (764/A, 22nd Cross, Parangipalya Main Road, 2nd Sector, HSR Layout, Bengaluru 560102). We message you when it is ready.

## Prescription medicines

Prescription-only items are dispatched only after we have checked your vet's prescription.

## Problems with a delivery

If your order is late, damaged or wrong, message us on WhatsApp. See our [Cancellation & Refund Policy](/cancellation-refund-policy/).
